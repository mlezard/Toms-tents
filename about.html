About Tom's Tents

Welcome to Tom’s Tents! My name’s Tom, and I run this shop with my granddaughter Campette.

We started 5 years ago, when I noticed our lovely town of Chichester had no camping shop. Since then, we’ve become the number 4 independent camping shop in Chichester.

If you want to buy some equipment or get expert advice, we are the place for you.
