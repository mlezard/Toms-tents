Tom's Tents - Workshops

Workshops

Here at Tom's Tents, we've been hiking, camping, and exploring the great outdoors for more than 30 years...so why not make use of our expertise? Every week, we run in-person workshops from our high street shop, facilitated by Tom or a local expert.

20/11/26 - Firemaking Workshop

Learn how to light campfires outdoors using a fire striker. We will provide all materials and equipment.

2 hours - £30 per person

27/11/26 - Map Reading Workshop

Although we all have GPS on our phones nowadays, orienteering using a paper map is still an essential skill. We'll learn how to use a compass to find our way around, including short excursion around town.

3 hours - £40 per person

4/12/26 - First Aid Workshop

Basic first aid is one of the most useful skills you can have, especially for treating minor injuries when you're out in the wilderness.

3 hours - £25 per person
